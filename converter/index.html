Free File Converters

Convert images, PDFs and spreadsheets — free, with no account and no catch.

🔒 Your files never leave your computer. Every conversion happens right here in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to a server, nothing is stored, and there is nothing to sign up for. Close the tab and your files are exactly where they always were — on your machine.

Everyday converters

JPG → PNG

Turn a JPG photo into a PNG image.

PNG → JPG

Turn a PNG into a JPG. Transparent areas are placed on a white background, because JPG has no transparency.

WEBP → PNG or JPG

Turn a WEBP image (the kind websites serve) into a PNG or JPG you can use anywhere.

Images → PDF

Put one or more images (JPG, PNG, WEBP) into a single PDF, one image per page, in the order you pick them.

PDF → JPG or PNG

Turn every page of a PDF into its own picture. Each page downloads as a separate image file.

Merge PDF

Combine two or more PDFs into one file, in the order you pick them.

Split PDF

Pull pages out of a PDF. Either save a page range (like 1-3, 5) as one new PDF, or save every page as its own PDF.

Compress / Resize Image

Shrink a JPG, PNG or WEBP: cap its size in pixels and, for JPG, dial the quality. The result shows the real before-and-after file sizes — no made-up percentages.

Office & paperwork converters

Excel → CSV

Turn an Excel sheet (.xlsx or .xls) into a plain CSV file. Pick which sheet to convert after you choose the file — formulas are exported as the values they last calculated to.

CSV → Excel

Turn a CSV file into a proper Excel workbook (.xlsx) you can open, sort and format in Excel or Google Sheets.

Excel → PDF (simple table)

Print one sheet of an Excel file to a clean, simple PDF table. Best for lists, logs and registers — very wide sheets get trimmed to fit the page, so check the preview numbers before you rely on it.

What this hub does — and doesn't do

Everything above genuinely works, and every tool on this page was tested on real files before it was listed here. You will not find "PDF to Word" or "Word to PDF" on this page: a web browser cannot do those conversions honestly at real quality, so we don't fake them or dress up plain text extraction as a Word document. If a conversion can't be done properly right here in your browser, we'd rather tell you that than hand you a broken file.
